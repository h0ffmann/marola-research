# The bench is the score series

"The bench are the score series performed by any Forecaster, Registered by an Actor." And: "The
bench is the final goal."

A score is one forecast compared with one observation. A series is those scores in order of
time, for one forecaster, one point, one variable and one lead. The bench is all the series.

Two things follow. marola's own forecaster is one forecaster among the others, scored by the
same rule. And a series is a record: a score that was registered is not edited.
