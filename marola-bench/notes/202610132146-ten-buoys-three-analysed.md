# Ten buoys, three analysed

The abstract describes "a dense network of buoys along the Brazilian coast, equipped with
several meteorological and oceanographic sensors. Out of ten currently operational buoys, three
are employed to present the main characteristics of waves in the Southern part of the network."

For the bench it means that the truth is thin. The accepted design counts at most about nine
buoys at the same time, and expects most cells to have too few cases for months.
